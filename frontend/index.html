About NutriScan AI

NutriScan AI is an intelligent food analysis platform that combines deep learning, computer vision, nutrition data, and generative AI to help users understand what they eat.

Simply upload a food image and NutriScan AI identifies the food, estimates its typical serving weight, calculates nutritional values, and provides personalized nutrition guidance through an AI assistant.

How NutriScan AI Works

1. Upload
Upload a clear image of your food.
2. Identify
The deep learning model analyzes the image and predicts the food category.
3. Estimate
NutriScan estimates a typical serving weight and scales the nutrition values accordingly.
4. Ask AI
Ask nutrition-related questions and receive AI-generated guidance.

Supported Foods

NutriScan AI can analyze a growing collection of fruits, vegetables, Indian foods, meals, snacks, and other common foods.
